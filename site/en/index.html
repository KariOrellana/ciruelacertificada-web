Sustainability standards

What do they consist of?

The suggested standards include Good Practices with their corresponding actions classified into 5 Dimensions

Environment

Water, soil, biodiversity, inputs, residues, energy, greenhouse gases.

Quality

Quality Management, Safety Management.

ECONOMIC VIABILITY

Economic viability

Social

Local Communities, Working Conditions and Social Protection.

Ethics

Due diligence of the law.

Each of the actions that include the standard for primary production as well as for the agro-industrial adequacy is classified into 4 levels: fundamental, basic, intermediate and advanced.

Good practices and actions

Classification levels

The proposal for Primary Production and for Agro-industrial Adjustment contemplates different levels of evaluation, which are divided into:
Fundamental

Actions that regardless of their level of complexity of implementation and impact on sustainability, are considered very relevant to qualify a company as sustainable. The level of mandatory nature of these actions is higher than the rest of the categories.

Basic

Actions with less impact on sustainability and less complexity of implementation.

Intermediate

Actions with sustainability impact and intermediate implementation complexity.

Advanced

Actions with greater impact on sustainability and greater complexity of implementation.

Based on their level of prioritization, the actions are classified and assigned a specific score. Thus, the standard allows a company to obtain a maximum score based on the actions it complies with at the time of the audit.
